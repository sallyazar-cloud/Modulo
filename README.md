# Modulo

Colorful, browser-based English worksheets organized into six subject areas for Grades 1–5 and four for the other grade levels.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser to visit the hub. No installation, account, or internet connection is needed.

The top-left **Classes & subjects** menu is the main navigation: expand KG 1–KG 3 or Grades 1–9 to find that class's activities. Grades 1–9 include Vocabulary, Grammar, Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing; KG levels retain Vocabulary, Grammar, Reading, and Writing.

Grade 1 lessons follow the workbook's CP plan, Grade 2 follows CE1, and Grade 3 follows CE2. Grade 4 follows CM1 and Grade 5 follows CM2 in the supplied French programme for foreign and regional languages. Each CM grade covers all 18 competency strands across five periods. Grade 6 maps to sixième, Grade 7 to cinquième, Grade 8 to quatrième, and Grade 9 to troisième in the supplied collège English programme. Their periods follow the cultural axes and display the stage's CEFR targets, objectives, success criteria, suggested tasks, and competencies for listening, reading, speaking, interaction and mediation, and writing. Grades 7–9 include the relevant required country focus: the United Kingdom, Ireland, or the United States. Listening tasks can be played aloud.

The home page introduces Modulo and points to the Grade 6 resource library, where the alphabet, colors, school supplies, and classroom commands are grouped under Vocabulary, and the basket-of-fruits story is under Reading. These featured standalone worksheets are linked from Grade 6 only; the grade-specific activity menus remain available for every class. Grade 6 Grammar teaches have/has in Period 1 (people and characters), with a link to the full timed grammar practice.

The seasonal Halloween look is isolated in `assets/halloween-theme.css`, with its artwork in `assets/halloween-season.jfif`, so the theme can be updated or removed later without changing worksheet content.

## Worksheets

- **Basics & Vocabulary:** [The Alphabet](./alphabet.html), [Colors](./colors.html), [School Supplies](./school-supplies.html), and [Classroom Language: Commands & Actions](./classroom-language.html). Each has clearly separated Easy, Medium, and Hard levels, timed question sets and matching games, animated feedback, and drag-and-drop matching with a click-to-match alternative.
- **Grade 6 Grammar:** Period 2 covers have/has for people, family, and possessions, with a link to the full timed practice for positive forms, negatives, and questions.
- **Reading Comprehension:** [The Basket of Colorful Fruits](./reading/basket-of-fruits.html) — an original illustrated story, a timed fruit picture matching game, and a comprehension quiz with clearly separated Easy, Medium, and Hard levels and answer feedback.
- **Writing:** the grade activity pages include age-progressive writing prompts with space to draft a response.

The interactive worksheets use short sets, clear instructions, instant feedback, progress tracking, and an optional one-question-at-a-time mode. Grade activities offer clearly separated Easy, Medium, and Hard challenge levels, timed practice, and retry feedback. The site uses the locally hosted Andika typeface, with a clearer Arial capital `I` glyph, distributed under the SIL Open Font License in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

# Modulo

Colorful, browser-based English worksheets organized into six subject areas for Grades 1–5 and four for the other grade levels.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser to visit the hub. No installation, account, or internet connection is needed.

The top-left **Classes & subjects** menu is the main navigation: expand KG 1–KG 3 or Grades 1–9 to find that class's activities. Grades 1–5 include Vocabulary, Grammar, Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing; other grades retain Vocabulary, Grammar, Reading, and Writing.

Grade 1 lessons follow the workbook's CP plan, Grade 2 follows CE1, and Grade 3 follows CE2. Grades 4–5 extend CE2 progressively. Grade 1–5 lesson pages include five period plans, learning objectives, success criteria, and suggested tasks; listening tasks can be played aloud.

The seasonal Halloween look is isolated in `assets/halloween-theme.css`, with its artwork in `assets/halloween-season.jfif`, so the theme can be updated or removed later without changing worksheet content.

## Worksheets

- **Basics & Vocabulary:** [The Alphabet](./alphabet.html), [Colors](./colors.html), [School Supplies](./school-supplies.html), and [Classroom Language: Commands & Actions](./classroom-language.html). All have Easy: The Curious Researcher, Medium: The Keen Observer, and Hard: The Language Guru levels, timed question sets and matching games, animated feedback, and drag-and-drop matching with a click-to-match alternative.
- **Grammar Lab:** [Verb To Have](./grammar/verb-to-have.html) — present-simple have/has forms, negatives, and questions, with the same three named timed levels and answer feedback.
- **Reading Comprehension:** [The Basket of Colorful Fruits](./reading/basket-of-fruits.html) — an original illustrated story, a timed fruit picture matching game, and a comprehension quiz with the same three named timed levels and answer feedback.
- **Writing:** the grade activity pages include age-progressive writing prompts with space to draft a response.

The interactive worksheets use short sets, clear instructions, instant feedback, progress tracking, and an optional one-question-at-a-time mode. Grade activities offer three named challenge levels, timed practice, and retry feedback. The site uses the locally hosted Andika typeface, with a clearer Arial capital `I` glyph, distributed under the SIL Open Font License in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
